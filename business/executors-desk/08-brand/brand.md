# Brand

**Name:** The Executor's Desk
**Domain to register:** theexecutorsdesk.com (check availability; fallbacks: executorsdesk.co, theexecutorsdesk.co). If the primary is taken, do not add hyphens; pick a fallback.
**Tagline (use sparingly):** The first 90 days, in order.
**Email:** help@ (support and sender), no "noreply".
**Colors:** ink #1d1d1b · accent #2f4f4f · rule #c9c5bb · paper #ffffff · warm box #faf8f3
**Type:** Charter / Georgia for body; Liberation Sans / Helvetica / Inter for headings and labels.
**Logo:** wordmark only, all caps, letter-spaced, in the accent color. No icon. No desk illustration.
**Photography:** printed pages on a plain desk, natural light, a pen, a stack of mail. Never a stock "grieving woman."
**Product naming:** every product is a plain noun that says what it is. The First 90 Days. Scripts & Letters Pack. The Estate Ledger. State Addendum: Texas.
**Legal line, everywhere:** "General information, not legal advice. Verify state rules with your county probate court."
