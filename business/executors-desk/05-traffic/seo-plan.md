# SEO plan

The blog lives on the funnel domain. Every article answers one question an executor types into Google at night, in the voice guide, and ends with the `/72` opt-in block. The articles are the long-term traffic engine; Google Ads is the short-term one, and the two share keywords.

## Publishing cadence
- Launch with A1, A2, A3 (finished drafts in `articles/`).
- One article per week for 24 weeks from the briefs below. The automation in `07-automation` drafts each one from its brief on schedule for the owner to approve; nothing publishes unreviewed.
- Every article: 1,200 to 1,800 words, H2s phrased as questions, one table, one "what the notebook adds" box, the opt-in block, the legal line.

## On-page checklist (every article)
- Title tag under 60 characters with the keyword first
- Meta description 150 characters, plain, includes the free checklist
- URL slug: the keyword, hyphenated, no dates
- First paragraph answers the question in two sentences
- Internal links: two to other articles, one to `/72`, one to `/notebook`
- Schema: Article + FAQPage for the question H2s
- Author box with the real name and the "not legal advice" line
- Image: a photo of the relevant notebook page, alt text describing it

## Off-page (slow, optional, honest)
- Answer questions on r/EstatePlanning and r/personalfinance with real answers; link only when someone asks for a resource.
- Offer the free checklist to hospice social workers and funeral homes as a printable handout with the URL on it. This is the single best distribution channel in the niche and it costs postage.
