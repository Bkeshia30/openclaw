# Pinterest plan

Pinterest is the right organic channel for this buyer: half of US women use it, users are in a planning mindset, and pins keep sending traffic for months. It is slow (expect three to six months to meaningful traffic) and it costs nothing but scheduling.

## Account
- Business account named "The Executor's Desk." Claim the website. Enable Rich Pins (Article and Product).
- Profile line: "Practical checklists and tools for settling a parent's estate. The first 90 days, in order."

## Pin format
- 1000 × 1500 px (2:3). One typeface pair (Charter + a plain sans). Ink text on off-white, accent #2f4f4f. No stock photos of candles, hands or sunsets; use a photograph of the printed page or plain type.
- Title on the pin in 6 to 9 words. The pin title field: the keyword phrase. Description: two plain sentences plus the destination.
- 70% of pins link to `/72` (free checklist), 20% to the relevant article, 10% to the Etsy listing.
- Cadence: 5 to 7 new pins per week, spread across boards, scheduled through the automation in `07-automation` (Pinterest API or Tailwind). Fresh images matter more than volume.
