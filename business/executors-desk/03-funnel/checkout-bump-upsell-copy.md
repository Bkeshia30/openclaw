**Top bar:** Your notebook is paid for and waiting on the next page. One thing before you go.

**Headline:** The notebook ends at day 90 on purpose. This is what handles days 91 to 480.

**Body:**
Settling an estate takes about 16 months on average. After the first 90 days the work changes: it becomes a ledger. Every dollar into the estate account and every dollar out has to be categorized, reconciled and, at the end, turned into an accounting that beneficiaries or a court will accept.

Most executors do this in a notes app and a checking-account statement, and then spend a weekend at month fourteen rebuilding it from receipts.

**The Estate Ledger** is a Google Sheets template built for that job:
- Inventory tab that matches the notebook's A/B/C sorting, with totals by category
- Ledger tab with categories a probate accounting expects, a running balance, and a receipts checkbox that turns rows amber until you attach one
- Claims tab with your creditor-window date; anything received after it turns red
- Distribution tab that computes each beneficiary's share from the ledger and tracks partials and releases
- A print-ready final accounting page that pulls from all of it
- A 12-minute walkthrough video, no face, no fluff
