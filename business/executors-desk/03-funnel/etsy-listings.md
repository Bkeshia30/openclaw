Somebody named you executor and nobody told you what to do first. This printable workbook lays out the first 90 days after a parent dies, in order: the 72-hour list, week one, getting appointed by the court, and the deadlines that live in days 30 to 90.

• 7 phone scripts: bank (before and after Letters), credit card, Social Security, utilities, life insurer, home insurer

Adult children (and spouses, siblings, friends) settling an estate in the United States, with or without a lawyer. Works for executors, administrators and, for most sections, successor trustees.

HOW IT WORKS
Instant download after purchase. Open in any PDF app. Print the tracker pages as many times as you need for one estate.

PLEASE NOTE
This is general information and practical tools, not legal advice. Probate rules and dollar limits vary by state and change; the workbook tells you where to verify each one for free.
